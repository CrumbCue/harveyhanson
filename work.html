Campaign concept: 7 days of small wins

Wellness / fitness brand.

Objective

Encourage saves, replies, and participation through practical daily prompts.

Audience

People who want to build realistic, sustainable wellness habits.

Message

Progress comes from small, repeatable actions, not perfection.

Content sequence

  1. Day 1 Set a simple goal
  2. Day 2 Share a practical tip
  3. Day 3 Invite a check-in
  4. Day 4 Demonstrate a habit
  5. Day 5 Ask a community question
  6. Day 6 Share a short how-to video
  7. Day 7 Reflect and celebrate progress

How I would measure it

Track reach, saves, shares, comments, story replies, and profile visits. Compare results with the campaign baseline before drawing conclusions.
